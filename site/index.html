Musical mapping

Azimuth → circle-of-fifths root. Elevation → mode. Brighter V magnitude → higher, scale-quantised pitch. Cursor proximity → attack velocity.

Colour CC is for a synth or electronics layer. It does not change an acoustic piano's timbre. Unknown colour sends no CC.

Observer & time

Editable city-level default: Vienna. This sets physical sky elevation/azimuth, not screen x/y.

Approximate geometric Alt/Az; no refraction or proper-motion correction. Below-horizon objects remain visible in this atlas.

Coordinates & export

Exports contain cursor coordinates, timestamps, object/musical events and settings, not webcam frames. Keep participant recordings private.
